## Sequence overview

| # | Day | Angle | Goal |
|---|-----|-------|------|
| 1 | 0 | Renewal / license lapse risk | Open + reply |
| 2 | 3 | Inspection-unprepared pain | Soft CTA |
| 3 | 7 | Multi-framework complexity (wedge) | Book demo |
| 4 | 12 | Concrete value drop (checklist) | Reply or trial |
| 5 | 18 | Breakup | Last chance / clean exit |

**Rules**
- Personalize line 1 with state, license type, or something real (new location, hiring injector, Google review mentioning “medical director,” etc.).
- Keep under ~90 words when possible. One ask per email.
- Never claim Aesthrx files or renews on their behalf — it monitors, reminds, and prep-checks.
- Prefer “owner / operator / clinical director” over generic “team.”

- `{{FirstName}}` · `{{SpaName}}` · `{{State}}` · `{{City}}` · optional `{{Hook}}` (personalization)

## Send settings (recommended)

- **Volume:** Start 20–40 highly personalized / day before scaling.
- **Cadence:** Days 0 / 3 / 7 / 12 / 18 (adjust ±1 for weekends).
- **From name:** Founder or “{{Name}} from Aesthrx” — not “Compliance Team.”
- **Tracking:** Log subject variant, state, location count, reply reason in your outreach tracker.
- **Stop on reply:** Immediate — human take over.
