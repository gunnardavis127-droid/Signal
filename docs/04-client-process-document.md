# Signal — Client-Facing Process Document

## How We Build Your Site

Every Signal project runs through five phases. This isn't flexible — it's what makes the work predictable, fast, and exceptional. Here's exactly what to expect.

---

### Phase 1: Intake (Week 1)

**What happens:**
We start with a discovery session (60–90 minutes) where we dig into your business goals, your audience, what's working right now, and what isn't. You'll get a short questionnaire beforehand so we come in prepared.

**What we need from you:**
- Brand assets: logos, brand colors, font preferences, any existing brand guidelines
- Content: existing copy, messaging, and any materials you want incorporated
- Access: logins for your current site, domain registrar, hosting, and analytics
- Decision: who on your team owns approvals? We need one point of contact with authority to say "yes."

**What you get from us:**
- A signed agreement with scope, timeline, and investment clearly stated
- An invoice for the 50% deposit (work begins when this is paid)
- A shared project folder where all assets, milestones, and deliverables live

**Phase 1 is complete when:** deposit is received, all assets are collected, and the discovery session is done.

---

### Phase 2: Planning (Week 2)

**What happens:**
Before we design a single pixel, we map the entire user journey. We build a sitemap showing every page and how they connect, define the primary calls-to-action and conversion paths, and lock every technical decision (hosting, CMS, integrations).

**What we need from you:**
- Feedback on the sitemap and user journey — we'll walk you through it on a 30-minute call
- Sign-off on the milestone timeline: design review → development → final review → launch

**What you get from us:**
- A sitemap document showing the full page hierarchy
- A milestone timeline with specific dates for each checkpoint
- A technical spec: hosting, CMS, any third-party integrations, and how they'll work

**Phase 2 is complete when:** you sign off on the sitemap, timeline, and technical decisions.

---

### Phase 3: Build (Weeks 3–6)

**What happens:**
This is where the work happens. We build one section at a time — not the whole site at once — so you can see real progress at each checkpoint. Every section is designed, built responsive, and wired with motion and interactions before we move to the next.

**What we need from you:**
- Timely feedback at each checkpoint (48-hour turnaround keeps us on schedule)
- Content for any pages we've agreed you'll provide
- Decisions made by your designated point of contact

**What you get from us:**
- A staging link updated at each checkpoint (you'll see the site come together)
- Checkpoint reviews where we walk through what was built and confirm direction
- No surprises: the scope we agreed on in Phase 2 is what gets built. Changes after sign-off go into a separate scope conversation.

**Phase 3 is complete when:** every page and section from the sitemap is built and on staging.

---

### Phase 4: Revision & Refinement (Week 7)

**What happens:**
We review the complete site against a 25-point quality checklist: responsiveness across devices, load speed, spacing and typography consistency, animation polish, accessibility, SEO fundamentals, working forms, analytics, and cross-browser testing.

You get **two rounds of revisions** (this is the limit — it prevents endless tweaking and keeps us on timeline). Each round is a consolidated list of changes from your review. We apply them, you confirm.

**What we need from you:**
- Round 1 feedback: a single consolidated list, not rolling emails
- Round 2 feedback: final adjustments only — this is the last pass
- Sign-off after Round 2

**What you get from us:**
- The completed staging site, QA'd and ready
- A revision log showing every change applied per round

**Phase 4 is complete when:** you sign off on the final staging site.

---

### Phase 5: Delivery (Week 8)

**What happens:**
We go live. We handle DNS, deployment, SSL, and post-launch monitoring. Then we walk you through the finished site, provide documentation for anything you'll manage yourself (CMS, content updates), and hand over all credentials and assets.

**What we need from you:**
- Final payment (remaining 50% due before go-live)
- Domain/DNS access if we're managing the launch
- Your availability for a 60-minute walkthrough and training session

**What you get from us:**
- Your live site, monitored for the first 72 hours
- A training session for your team (CMS, analytics, content updates)
- A handover document with all logins, hosting details, and maintenance notes
- A [30/60/90]-day support window starting from launch day
- A post-launch strategy session (scheduled ~30 days out to review performance)

**Phase 5 is complete when:** the site is live, training is done, and you have the handover document.

| Phase | Duration | Key Milestone |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| Intake | Week 1 | Deposit paid, assets collected |
| Planning | Week 2 | Sitemap & timeline signed off |
| Build | Weeks 3–6 | Checkpoint reviews, staging updated |

**Total: ~8 weeks.** Your fast feedback at each checkpoint is what keeps us on this timeline. Delays in feedback push delivery — we'll communicate any timeline shifts immediately.
