# Signal — Outreach Message Templates (Financial Advisors)

## Structure (always 3 parts)
**Context** → something specific and genuine noticed about their business/site → **Value** → one sentence framing how we could help → **Question** → soft, low-pressure invitation to respond

## Template A: Outdated Website (LinkedIn DM)
```
[Name], I came across [Firm Name] after seeing your recent [post/article/seminar] on [topic]. 

Your expertise is obvious — but your website isn't telling that story. I help financial advisors turn their site into a client acquisition engine, and I'd love to send you a quick mockup of what a modern refresh could look like.

Would that be useful?
```
**When to use:** Advisor has an obviously outdated site but is active on LinkedIn posting good content.

## Template B: Missing CTA / Passive Site (Email)
```
Subject: [Firm Name]'s website — a quick thought

[Name],

I was looking at your site after hearing about [Firm Name] through [source/reference]. Your team clearly knows wealth management — but I noticed there's no clear path for a prospective client to book a consultation.

I help RIAs and financial planners turn their website into the firm's #1 source of qualified prospects. Happy to share a few specific ideas for [Firm Name]'s site if you're open to it.

No pitch — just a second set of eyes from someone who does this every day. Worth a quick chat?
```
**When to use:** Advisor has a professional-looking site but no clear conversion path, no prominent CTA, or a buried contact form.

## Template C: Template / Generic Site (LinkedIn or Email)
```
[Name],

I noticed [Firm Name] is growing — [specific signal: new office, new hire, LinkedIn growth, recent press]. That kind of growth usually means your website needs to work harder than a templated design allows.

I help financial advisory firms build sites that actually convert visitors into consultation bookings — not just look professional. Would you be open to seeing what a custom approach could look like for [Firm Name]?

No pressure, just curious if it's on your radar.
```
**When to use:** Firm is clearly growing but using a generic template (Wealthbox, Advisor Websites, FMG Suite, etc.).

## Template D: Referral / Seminar Signal (Email)
```
Subject: Saw your upcoming seminar — quick idea

[Name],

I saw you're hosting a [seminar/webinar/dinner event] on [topic]. Smart move — those events work, but most advisors lose 80% of the attendees because their website doesn't close the loop afterward.

I help advisors fix that gap. If you're open to it, I'd love to show you how we'd turn [Firm Name]'s site into the follow-up engine that converts seminar attendees into consultation bookings.

Worth 15 minutes?
```
**When to use:** Advisor is actively running seminars/events — the site-to-event pipeline is a concrete, valuable angle.

---

## Message Rules (always follow)
- Read their site first. Every time. The context line must reference something real.
- Keep it 3-4 sentences broken into scannable lines. Never a wall of text.
- LinkedIn: slightly more casual tone. Email: professional but still personal.
- Never pitch in the first message. The goal is starting a conversation.
- Follow up once after 5 days if no response: "Just checking you saw my note — no pressure either way."
- Track everything. Response rate, which template worked, what got ignored.
