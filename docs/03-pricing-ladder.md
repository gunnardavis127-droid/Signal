# Signal — Pricing Ladder Rollout Plan

## Core Principle
**Value-based pricing only. Never hourly.** Price is tied to the business outcome created for the client, not time spent or page count. State price as a single confident statement tied to ROI — then stop talking.

---

## Tier 1: Proof of Capability — $1,000–$2,000/project

**When you're here:** No paid client work yet (or only pro-bono/personal projects). You need to prove the system works with real clients.

**What you deliver at this tier:**
- 3–5 page site (starter tier from the pricing structure)
- Light customization from the component library
- Client-provided copy (you're not writing it — position this as "you know your business best, I'll make it look exceptional")
- Basic on-page SEO + analytics setup
- 14-day post-launch support

**What you're really doing here:** Building portfolio pieces, collecting testimonials, and stress-testing the delivery system. Every project should produce a case study. Price is intentionally low to remove friction — not because the work is worth less, but because you're buying reference assets.

**Proof points to move up:**
- [ ] 2–3 completed projects with happy clients
- [ ] 2+ written testimonials
- [ ] The delivery system runs smoothly (you're not scrambling)
- [ ] At least one client result you can cite specifically ("increased consultation bookings by 40%")

---

## Tier 2: Growth — $5,000–$10,000/project

**When you're here:** You have a real portfolio, the system is proven, and you're solving business problems — not just building sites.

**What you deliver at this tier:**
- 5–10 custom-designed pages
- Strong on-page SEO with keyword mapping
- Mapped user journey + site architecture document
- Lead capture system (forms, CTAs, conversion tracking)
- CMS integration (client can update content)
- 60-day support window + one post-launch strategy session
- Discovery/strategy phase priced and scoped separately from the build

**What shifts at this tier:**
- You lead with strategy, not design. The discovery phase is a line item.
- You're asking different questions in sales calls — not "what pages do you need?" but "what business problem are we solving?"
- Clients are businesses actively investing in growth (running ads, tracking metrics, have marketing spend).
- You stop justifying price entirely. The work speaks.

**Proof points to move up:**
- [ ] 5+ completed projects at this tier
- [ ] Prospects have stopped negotiating on price
- [ ] You're fully booked (pipeline > capacity)
- [ ] 2–3 strong case studies with measurable outcomes (revenue increases, lead growth, conversion improvements)
- [ ] Referrals are coming in without asking

---

## Tier 3: Transformation — $10,000–$25,000+/project

**When you're here:** You're selective. You turn down more than you accept. You're not "a web designer" — you're a strategic partner who happens to deliver through websites.

**What you deliver at this tier:**
- Full discovery engagement: competitive audit, user research, brand strategy, positioning document (this alone can be a $3K–$5K line item)
- Fully custom design/branding — the component library is the skeleton, but every skin is bespoke
- 10–20 pages with complete SEO strategy
- Professional copywriting (you bring in a specialist or partner)
- Advanced analytics, conversion tracking, and a reporting dashboard
- 90-day support transitioning into a monthly retainer ($1K–$3K/mo for ongoing optimization, content updates, analytics reviews)
- E-commerce or advanced functionality as needed
- 3D/WebGL features where they serve the narrative (not decoration)

**What shifts at this tier:**
- You're selling to businesses where one customer is worth $10K+. A $25K site that brings 3 new clients pays for itself immediately — that's the math you anchor to.
- The sales conversation is peer-to-peer. You qualify them as much as they qualify you.
- You walk away from bad-fit clients without hesitation.
- Your pipeline is referral-driven. Outbound becomes supplementary.

**Signals you're ready:**
- [ ] You've been at $5K–$10K for 6+ months
- [ ] Every project is fully booked 4–8 weeks out
- [ ] You have case studies showing direct revenue impact
- [ ] Clients treat you as a strategic advisor, not a vendor

---

## The Pricing Mindset (revisit before every sales call)
- Price is never "how long will this take?" — it's "what is this worth to the client's business?"
- Never itemize hours. Never apologize for price. Never offer a discount without removing scope.
- If someone balks at $5K: "I understand. At this level, the investment makes sense for businesses where the site directly drives revenue — if that's not where you are right now, I'm happy to recommend some great template options." Disqualify gracefully.
- Raise prices when: prospects stop negotiating, you're booked solid, projects feel easy, you have a stack of strong testimonials. Don't wait for permission.
